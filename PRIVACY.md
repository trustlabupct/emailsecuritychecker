## What we do
TrustEmail analyzes Gmail messages you open to highlight phishing and fraud risks. Analysis happens within your browser; we do not run a backend service for this extension.

## Data we access
- Gmail message headers and content (read-only) via the Gmail API when you open or analyze a thread.
- Local settings you configure (feature toggles, allow/block lists, ignored threads).
- Local analysis history (bounded to 1000 entries).

## How we use data
- Only to perform risk analysis and display results to you inside Gmail.
- Optional OCR/QR/NLP features run locally. Online lookups are off by default; if enabled, they still do not send email content to third parties.

## Storage and sharing
- Storage: chrome.storage.local on your device. Data is cleared on uninstall and can be cleared in Settings.
- Sharing: none with third parties. Network calls go only to Google’s Gmail API using your token.
- No sale or advertising use of your data.

## Security
- Runs within Chrome’s extension sandbox. We do not transfer your email data off your device.

## Children
- Not intended for children under 13.
